1. The software

Cirrove is open-source software licensed under the Apache License 2.0. It presents a cloud drive as a folder on your Linux computer. The source-code licence governs copying, modification and distribution; these terms describe the operated sign-in configuration, website and your use of the connected service.

2. Your cloud account and permission

You must have authority to connect the Google account and drive you select. Read-only mode can list and download content. If you enable Allow changes, ordinary-file saves, renames, moves and removals can change that Google Drive. Native Google Docs and Sheets are read-only export packages in this release.

3. Backups and preview limitations

Cirrove is pre-release software under validation. Keep an independent copy of data you cannot replace, review reported upload conflicts and do not treat an on-demand cache as a backup. Supported behavior and known limits are described in the public documentation for the version you install.

4. Acceptable use

Do not use Cirrove to access data without permission, evade provider limits, interfere with the service or violate applicable law. Your use of Google Drive also remains subject to Google's terms and the rules of the organization that owns a Shared Drive.

5. Privacy and removal

The privacy policy explains what Google data Cirrove accesses and keeps locally. The data-removal guide explains how to remove a connection, local state, keyring credentials and Google authorization.

6. Availability and changes

Features can depend on your operating system, desktop keyring, network, Google account permissions and Google APIs. These terms and the supported-version policy may change with a release; material changes will be dated on this page.
